## Founder's notebook-sensor idea (2026-10-26, after the v0.3 run, so it is NOT pre-results)
Board = a notebook of many boxes (sensors). Each box has an address (X, Y) plus a third value that combines
(a) a level (boxes inside boxes: zoom) and (b) something like time or strength. A marble's full position =
box address + its position inside the box. A box detects when a marble enters, from which side, and when,
which gives direction and speed: aimed at the v0.3 H5 failure (no "who moves first").
Open: what defines a marble's x/y.
